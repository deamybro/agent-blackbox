# Safer autonomous trading starts before execution

We built **Agent BlackBox** for the Bitget AI Base Camp Trading Infrastructure track.

It is a risk firewall and flight recorder for autonomous agents: every signal, reason, proposed trade, risk check, verdict, adjustment, and simulated outcome is persisted and inspectable. The firewall can allow, reduce, route for human review, or block an action before it reaches execution.

The product includes a working autonomous momentum agent, regime-aware risk scoring, persistent simulation evidence, audit drill-downs, reports, exports, and an integration-ready SDK.

Most importantly, it defaults to simulation only. Faster agents need stronger controls, clearer evidence, and a reliable way to answer one question: why did the agent want to do that?
